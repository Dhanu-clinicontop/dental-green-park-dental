FAQs

Questions patients ask us

Does a root canal hurt?

Modern RCT with proper anesthesia is largely painless — most patients compare it to getting a filling. We use rotary instruments that finish many cases in a single visit, so less time in the chair too.

How much will my treatment cost?

You get a written, itemized plan before anything begins. Consultation covers diagnosis and the quote — there are never surprise add-ons mid-treatment.

Can I get a same-day appointment for tooth pain?

Yes — we reserve same-day emergency slots every day. Message us on WhatsApp or call and mention "emergency" for the earliest slot.

Do you accept insurance?

We support reimbursement documentation for major insurers and can structure cashless options for eligible corporate plans — ask at reception with your policy details.
